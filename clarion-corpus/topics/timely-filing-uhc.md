# UHC timely filing & reconsideration window

The corpus's headline supersedes chain. [[docs/sop-timely-filing-appeals]] (Nov 2025) says 60 days from the remittance date. [[people/rachel-goldman]]'s Aug 5 call ([[expert_calls/2026-08-05-legal-call]], memo Aug 6) established **90 days per §7.3** for [[payers/unitedhealth]] delegated-credentialing contracts — which covers [[clients/harbor-family-medicine]]. Both Harbor reconsiderations (day 49 and day 72) were filed Aug 6.

SOP-007 has not been updated yet ([[people/elena-vasquez]], open, due Aug 31). Note: the HIPAA breach-notification 60-day window in [[docs/policy-compliance-hipaa]] is unrelated despite the matching number.
