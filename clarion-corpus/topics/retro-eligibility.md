# Retro-eligibility ([[payers/masshealth]])

When a claim denies for no coverage and the patient is later granted retroactive MassHealth eligibility: verify the retro span in EVS/MMIS, then submit a **new** claim (not an adjustment) within 90 days of the eligibility **determination** date. Since the 2026-07-01 bulletin, replacement claims also need delay-reason code 9 plus the determination letter attached.
