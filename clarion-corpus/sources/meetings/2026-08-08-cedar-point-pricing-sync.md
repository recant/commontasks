# Cedar Point pricing sync — 2026-08-08

**Attendees:** Wanda (chair), Dana, Grace, Sofia
**Purpose:** [[clients/cedar-point-pediatrics]] renewal (contract ends Nov 1). They rejected the initial +12% proposal; counter-offer strategy needed.
**Notes:** Wanda

## 1. Where the negotiation stands (Dana)

Their practice administrator called Monday: the +12% proposal ($0.94/claim) is "not happening," and they've relayed a competing quote at **$0.71 per claim** — 15% below even our current price. Tone is negotiating-but-warm: eleven months of good service, no operational complaints, this is a price conversation, not a quality one.

## 2. The account economics (Wanda)

- Volume: **41,200 claims/year**, current blended rate **$0.84/claim** (~$34.6k ARR).
- Gross margin on the account: **62%** — pediatric claims are clean and cheap to process.
- Support load: **0.4 tickets per 1,000 claims**, the lowest in the book (Sofia's data).
- Quality: **94.1% first-pass acceptance** — their clean claims are part of why the margin is good.

The churn math, plainly: at 62% margin, even renewing **flat** earns more than replacing this volume — sales cost plus onboarding plus ramp on a replacement logo exceeds three years of any concession we'd make here. Losing a healthy account over single-digit percentage points would be economically illiterate.

## 3. On the competitor quote (Wanda)

$0.71 against our cost structure would be a money-loser on their claim mix — a competitor can only sustain it as an intro rate with year-two step-ups, or by skipping the service layer (the digests, the training, the humans). We should decline to match it and say why without trash talk: "we're priced for the service level you've experienced."

## 4. Strategy agreed

Counter at **+6% ($0.89) with a 2-year term**, and **include the vaccine-inventory billing module** (lists at $450/mo) at no charge — high perceived value for a pediatric practice, low marginal cost to us, and it makes our offer structurally different from a bare per-claim price. Sofia to prep a one-pager of their own quality stats for the packet — let the 94.1% argue for us.

**Grace — floor decision:** approved as proposed, and the **walk-away floor is +4%** with the module included. Below that, she'd rather take the churn risk than reprice the book: "every account watches what the squeaky one gets."
