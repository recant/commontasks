**Question presented.** Whether UnitedHealthcare's standard 60-day reconsideration window applies to claims arising under Clarion clients' delegated-credentialing participation agreements.

**Short answer.** No. Section 7.3 ("Provider Disputes and Reconsiderations") of the delegated-credentialing participation agreement provides that reconsideration requests may be submitted **within ninety (90) days of the date of the remittance advice**. As negotiated contract language, §7.3 controls over the 60-day default in UHC's provider administrative manual, which governs only non-delegated participation.

**Application.** Harbor Family Medicine participates under the delegated form. CLM-2026-19882 (remittance dated 2026-05-26) is at day 72 as of today's filing — **timely under §7.3**. CLM-2026-20417 (remittance 2026-06-18, day 49) is timely under either standard. File both through the delegated-portal dispute queue with §7.3 cited in the cover letter; the standard queue may auto-apply the 60-day timer.
