# Compliance Policy: HIPAA & Data Handling

**Effective:** 2026-01-15 · **Owner:** [[people/elena-vasquez]] · **Review cycle:** annual

## 1. Minimum necessary

Access, use, and disclose only the minimum necessary PHI for the task at hand. Client claim data never leaves the production environment; screenshots containing PHI are prohibited in Slack and tickets — reference claim IDs instead.

## 2. Business associate agreements

A signed BAA must be in place before any PHI is exchanged with a client, payer intermediary, or subprocessor. New data flows (including pilots) require a BAA review by [[people/elena-vasquez]] before go-live.

## 3. Breach response

Suspected incidents go to compliance@clarionhealth.example immediately. If a breach of unsecured PHI is confirmed, affected individuals must be notified within 60 days of discovery, per the HIPAA Breach Notification Rule; HHS and media notification thresholds are handled by compliance counsel.

> Note: this 60-day breach-notification window is a HIPAA requirement and has nothing to do with payer claim-appeal deadlines (see [[docs/sop-timely-filing-appeals]] for those).

## 4. Training

Annual HIPAA training is mandatory for all staff; new hires complete it within 30 days of start. Compliance tracks completion.

## 5. Data retention

Claim and remittance data retain for 7 years per payer contract requirements; Slack export policy is managed separately by IT.
