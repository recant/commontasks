# Claims Ops New-Hire Onboarding Guide

**Updated:** 2026-06-30 for the July cohort · **Owner:** [[people/priya-nair]] · **Maintainer:** [[people/tom-reyes]]

Welcome to claims ops! This guide gets you productive in your first month.

## Week 1–2: shadow

New hires shadow [[people/tom-reyes]] on the denial work queue for the first two weeks — watch how remittances post, how CARCs route, and how tickets get worked end to end.

## Read these SOPs first

1. [[docs/sop-claims-intake-scrubbing]] — how claims enter and get scrubbed
2. [[docs/sop-denial-management]] — the CARC triage table you'll live in
3. [[docs/sop-timely-filing-appeals]] — deadlines per payer
4. [[docs/sop-claim-attachments]] — pend handling

Heads up: SOPs cover most standard workflows, but plenty of payer-specific tribal knowledge isn't written down yet — when a denial pattern doesn't match the SOP, ask in #claims-ops rather than guessing. [[people/priya-nair]] is the escalation point for payer-behavior questions.

## Tools

- Work queue + ticketing: Clarion console (your manager grants access day 1)
- Eligibility lookups: payer portals + the console's 270/271 panel
- The AI assistant (Clarion Copilot) can summarize SOPs and past tickets — treat its answers as pointers, and verify anything deadline-related against the SOP or a human.

## People to know

[[people/priya-nair]] (payer behavior), [[people/sofia-chen]] (reports), [[people/jake-osei]] (platform/configs), [[people/dana-ortiz]] (client relationships), [[people/elena-vasquez]] (compliance).

## Your first-month checklist

HIPAA training (within 30 days, see [[docs/policy-compliance-hipaa]]), console access, shadow sign-off from Tom, first solo queue day in week 3, and a 30-day check-in with your manager.
