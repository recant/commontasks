# Clarion PTO & Leave Policy (2026)

**Effective:** 2026-01-05 · **Owner:** [[people/wanda-liu]]

## Allowance

All full-time employees accrue 15 days of PTO per year, plus company holidays. Up to 5 unused days carry over into the following year; carryover days expire March 31.

## Requesting time off

Request PTO in Rippling at least 2 weeks in advance for absences of 3+ days; shorter absences need manager approval only. Coverage is the requester's responsibility — name your coverage in the request (ops roles: post it in your team channel too).

## Sick and parental leave

Sick time is separate (10 days) and does not carry over. Parental leave is 12 weeks paid; talk to your manager and [[people/wanda-liu]] at least 60 days ahead where possible.

## Blackout guidance

No formal blackout dates, but ops asks that quarter-end weeks be avoided when the team is thin. Check the shared calendar before booking.
