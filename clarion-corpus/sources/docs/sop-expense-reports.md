# SOP-101: Expense Reports & Reimbursement

**Effective:** 2025-12-01 · **Owner:** [[people/wanda-liu]]

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense reports in Ramp within 30 days of the expense date. Itemized receipts are required for any expense over $25. Client-facing travel should be booked through the corporate travel portal where possible.

## Approval chain

Manager approval is required for all reports; expenses over $500 additionally require CFO approval from [[people/wanda-liu]]. Reimbursements pay out on the next semi-monthly payroll run after approval.

## Non-reimbursable

Alcohol beyond one drink at client dinners, upgrades, and anything without a business purpose noted. When unsure, ask #general before spending.

## Categories

Use the preset categories (Travel, Client Meals, Software, Conferences, Other). Miscategorized reports get bounced back, which delays everyone's payout batch — please just pick the right one.
