# UnitedHealthcare (UHC)

Commercial payer. The [[topics/timely-filing-uhc]] case: reconsideration window is **90 days** from the remittance date for clients on delegated-credentialing network agreements, per §7.3 of the participation agreement ([[expert_calls/2026-08-05-legal-call]], memo 2026-08-06). The 60-day figure in [[docs/sop-timely-filing-appeals]] applies only to standard non-delegated contracts and is stale for delegated clients like [[clients/harbor-family-medicine]].
